## Teachings / reasoning heard
- "Follow option price action first, not index" (index on 1-min shows noise; option is what you trade).
- Premium rises from IV (panic, falling mkts) or delta (absolute move). Slow market -> premiums crawl.
- IV skew: side where IV skew leans, market tries to move there (option-seller fear). Put IVs normally higher; equal call IV = calls relatively rich.
- Early-morning "sniper" scalps: fast, small SL, quick trailing. Avoid big risk in dull/sideways morning.
- Cluster / tight range: don't trade inside; wait for breakout + follow-through candle; avoid false breakout ("we trade the follow-up").
- Entry types: (a) breakout above zone + follow-up candle; (b) pullback ("first bounce") into zone with pin bar / green candle confirmation; (c) reversal only after W / rounding bottom / liquidity sweep — "never catch the bottom directly".
- Higher-high trading ok if risk small; inside candles -> stronger breakouts.
- SL placement: below zone / swing low / cluster low / single candle low. Move SL to breakeven ASAP after fresh high ("we won't give back ₹1"). Part-book + trail for multi-lot. Trail below each new higher low; never exit 100% at target, keep trailing piece.
