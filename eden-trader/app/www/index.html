Notifications

Every BUY / SELL call, book-half, exit, the morning plan and the day's result arrive as notifications, also when the app is closed. Keep them reliable: Settings > Apps > Eden Trader > Battery > Unrestricted.

A call counts as a success in History when the index went at least 50 points (Nifty) / 150 points (Sensex) its way before the stop loss. Calls are not advice; trade at your own risk.
